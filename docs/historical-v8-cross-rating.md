No cause is inferred. High-signal priority follows the five requested categories: overall-high/category-low, confirmed negative, textual issue despite high category score, unexplained low category score, mixed text. Other low-overall/high-category contradictions follow these at priority6. Review ID breaks ties deterministically. Quotes come from snapshot `analysis_text`; no author/identity/owner reply is added.
