V9 uses an isolated durable task ledger: one Phase1 overall Choice task and three unchanged Phase2 V7 theme tasks per textual review (388 evaluations for this source). State has only `review_alias` and `analysis_text`. Themes use the same25 questions, absent/positive/negative/both, `jev-latest`, concurrency8, mean probabilities and threshold0.50. Phase2B is never used. Textless reviews have no tasks, no themes and the existing rating-only overall fallback. `insufficient` or failed textual sentiment is unavailable, never repaired by Sol or stars. An incomplete3-repeat theme group is unavailable; successful partial responses remain auditable but are not averaged as though all3 succeeded.

Each request has the native client's15-second timeout and at most2 retries for429/5xx/temporary network errors, no401/422 retry. Paid usage is saved in the task ledger even when answer validation fails. Completed/failed tasks are never replayed. On a lost lease, interrupted requests have unknown paid outcomes and are marked unavailable/usage-incomplete instead of silently reissued. Worker claims cap at8 and stop starting waves after70 seconds; a final wave has bounded duration. Active elapsed time sums worker compute windows; end-to-end includes cron orchestration. New tables are RLS-enabled, raw client access revoked, service-only RPCs fence every write by parent worker/lease.

Cross-rating uses the unchanged V8 helper on Jev findings, preserves full-dataset Google averages and excludes unavailable theme rows from interpretive status counts/quoted high-signal cards. Price remains unrated. Manager diagnostics/thresholds are unchanged. High-signal text is the frozen English `analysis_text`.

There is zero Sol review-level extraction. The narrative receives a whitelist of anonymous counts, diagnostics, topic labels, priorities, cross-rating summaries and descriptive context; never reviews, original/analytical texts or evidence. Its output cannot alter structured counts/themes. Sol is pinned to `gpt-6.1-sol`, low effort, max3 summary sentences, FR/VI. A durable attempt marker permits at most one narrative request. Success/paid usage is checkpointed; failure/interruption/missing key uses deterministic assembly, never a Sol extraction or second narrative. Partial usage is labelled explicitly.
